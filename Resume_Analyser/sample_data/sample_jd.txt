Job Title: Senior Full-Stack & AI Engineer
Company: Apex Innovations
Location: Remote / Hybrid

About The Role:
Apex Innovations is seeking a talented Senior Full-Stack & AI Engineer to build next-generation intelligent platforms. You will lead the engineering of mission-critical cloud applications, develop AI-powered services, and collaborate closely with product and data engineering teams.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain robust, scalable APIs and microservices using Python and FastAPI or Django.
- Develop interactive, high-performance web frontends using React, TypeScript, and modern state management.
- Integrate AI / LLM capabilities, prompt workflows, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures into product workflows.
- Manage cloud infrastructure and CI/CD automation on AWS utilizing Docker and Kubernetes (k8s).
- Maintain high code quality through automated unit testing, TDD, code reviews, and continuous improvement.
- Partner with cross-functional stakeholders in an Agile/Scrum environment.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- 5+ years of experience in modern full-stack software development.
- Strong proficiency in Python and JavaScript/TypeScript.
- Hands-on experience with modern frontend frameworks (React, Next.js).
- Deep experience with backend frameworks (FastAPI, Flask, or Node.js) and REST APIs.
- Production experience with relational and NoSQL databases such as PostgreSQL and Redis.
- Solid understanding of Cloud & DevOps tooling: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience with Generative AI technologies, LangChain, or LLMs is highly desirable.
- Familiarity with Snowflake, Kafka, or gRPC is a plus.
- Excellent communication, problem solving, and mentorship skills.
